Getting help

Email hardwoodsapp@icloud.com. Say which phone you have and what you were doing when it went wrong, and a screenshot never hurts.

Common questions

Our house plays different rules. Open House Rules from the home screen. Round minimums, book values, red threes, the perfect-deal bonus, and the go-out bonus are all yours to change. Rules are saved with each game, so changing them later never rewrites an old score.

Someone got caught with a hand full of cards. Every scoring panel has a red Left in hand & foot section. Count what they're holding; it comes off their score, and a round can go negative.

We advanced a round and then found a mistake. Tap the ← beside the round number on the scoreboard to reopen the previous round. Fix the number, then tap Back to round. Later rounds are untouched.

Do I have enough to meld? Tap Need … to meld on the scoreboard. Count the cards you'd lay down and it tells you.

The text is too small. Hand & Foot follows your phone's text size. Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size → Larger Text.

Where is my data? On your phone, and nowhere else. See the privacy policy.
