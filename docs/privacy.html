HAND & FOOT

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026

The short version: Hand & Foot collects nothing. Everything you enter in the app stays on your phone.

What we collect

Nothing. Hand & Foot has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind. The app makes no network requests and sends no data — including player names, team names, or scores — to us or to anyone else.

Where your data lives

Every game, every round, your house rules, and your usual player names are stored locally on your phone only. Deleting the app deletes this data. We have no copy of it and no way to access it.

Sharing

When you export a game as a PDF, the app creates the file on your phone and hands it to the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes — by text, email, or any app you pick. Hand & Foot never uploads it anywhere itself.

Children

Hand & Foot is a scorekeeper for a family card game. Because the app collects and transmits nothing, no personal information about anyone — children included — is ever gathered by us.

Changes

If a future version of Hand & Foot ever changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated here before that version is released.
